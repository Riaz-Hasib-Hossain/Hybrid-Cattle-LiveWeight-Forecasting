# Dataset Access and Availability

## Dataset Description

The dataset supports research on animal-level cattle live-weight forecasting under irregular grazing observations.

The research includes automated livestock weighing observations, animal demographic information, and environmental measurements collected between 2022 and 2024.

Following preprocessing and quality control, the modelling dataset contains 683 cattle and 19,264 weekly records.
